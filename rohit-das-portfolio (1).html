About

Rohit Das — Diglipur, Andaman & Nicobar Islands

I'm a physics student currently reading for my MSc in Physics, with a background that runs from quantum mechanics and mathematical methods to the more observational end of the field — cosmology and the Cosmic Microwave Background. My degree work has taken me from the Andaman Islands, where I finished school and my diploma, to Punjab, where I'm now based for postgraduate study.

What draws me to physics is the back-and-forth between theory and instrument: reviewing the literature on why the CMB looks the way it does one week, then wiring up sensors and a microcontroller the next. I write mostly in Python and LaTeX, and I care about explaining physics clearly — teaching is part of where I want this to go.

A literature review covering the origin, anisotropies, blackbody spectrum, and significance of the CMB — surveying how this relic radiation constrains our picture of the early universe.
